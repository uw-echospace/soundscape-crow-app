Set the switch to USB/OFF
Slide the switch on the AudioMoth to USB/OFF if it isn't already set.

Put in batteries
Open the AudioMoth case and put in 3 recharged batteries.

Insert the SD card
Push a blank microSD card into the slot until it clicks.

Connect the AudioMoth
Connect the AudioMoth to this computer with the USB cable and adapter. If the battery voltage says REPLACE, put in new, recharged batteries first.

Flash the AudioMoth
Press Flash AudioMoth below and wait until it says it is finished. Do not unplug it while it is flashing.

Start recording
Unplug the USB cable and slide the switch to CUSTOM. Use the AudioMoth mobile chime app to start the recording. Ensure that the AudioMoth is recording before you leave your computer.

Deploy and take photos
Secure the AudioMoth in place at your location. With your phone's location turned on, take a location photo at arm's length from the AudioMoth. You can also take a reference photo from farther away showing the surroundings.

Collect the recordings
When the deployment is over, slide the switch to USB/OFF and take out the SD card. Then use the Detect Species tab.
